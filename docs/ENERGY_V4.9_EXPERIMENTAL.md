# Zero Theorem V4.9 — Energy Through Z0 (experimental)

## Purpose
Introduce an exploratory energy role into the existing Zero Theorem engine without replacing its mathematical foundation, REOC operation discovery, or established archives.

## Implementation
- Adds an observational energy sidecar at `<memory>.energy-pathways.json`.
- For each selected rational source construction, retains four **distinct** formal pathways: `+1`, `-1`, `+i`, `-i`.
- Retains source index, source ID, SHA-256 digest of the source record, expression tree, parents, ancestry, and available observer/space/time fields.
- Records blocked evaluations rather than dropping them.
- Processes up to 32 rational source records per save and persists a cursor for later continuation.
- Uses a distinct experimental memory filename: `zero_theorem_v49_energy_experimental_memory.json`.
- Leaves the prior memory save function in place, with energy observation following it.

- Existing five-domain zero-core passage coverage reported complete.

## Scope and limitations
This is a **formal computational experiment**, not a demonstration of physical energy, conservation laws, or a simulated physical universe. The current observer does not yet scan complex, symbolic, calculus, or roles archives for energy constructions. Energy records are not yet included in the engine's interactive `energy` / `energy-index` query parser or its main archive integrity audit. The four branches are formally defined transformations, not discoveries of physical interactions.

Preserve all mathematical and physical-pathway provenance, including blocked and distinct equal-result constructions. Do not promote candidate relationships into trusted laws without evidence.
