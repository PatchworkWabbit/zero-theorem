What this demonstrates

Zero Theorem explores constructions and retains relationships. If archive information is damaged, surviving evidence may support candidate reconstructions. This mock laboratory uses a fixed demonstration catalog; its candidate generation does not run the real engine's observers, zero nexus, task queues, complex operations, or recovery implementation.

An arithmetic expression being valid does not prove it was present in the original archive. When every record is lost, historical membership cannot be established from surviving records. The Regenerate from Z0 control illustrates a fresh exploration that creates new valid records without claiming to restore the original archive.
